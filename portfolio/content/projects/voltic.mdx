## Problem

Students and engineers across electrical, mechanical and electronics branches collect machine and sensor data, but turning it into analysis, conclusions and reports takes AI/ML skills most of them don't have.

## What I'm building

An AI layer for machine and sensor data:

- Upload experiment data, or connect a device live.
- AI agents analyze the data, answer questions about it, and draw conclusions.
- Detailed reports with stats and charts, tailored to each user.
- Auto-generated public showcase pages for project results, as a static snapshot or a live dashboard.

It starts with students and hobbyists, with industrial users as the longer-term target.

## Results / what I learned

Pitched at Eureka 2026, the startup competition run by E-Cell IIT Bombay. I led the VOLTIC team.
