## Problem

Manual roll calls waste teaching time, and proxy attendance breaks trust in the records.

## What I built

An open-source QR attendance platform for colleges that automates the whole workflow, from a student walking into the room to the final report.

- **Fast:** attendance is marked in under 10 seconds.
- **Anti-proxy:** QR codes rotate every 30 seconds, backed by a 3-layer check and proxy flagging.
- **Role-based:** separate dashboards for students, teachers, HODs and admins.
- **Secure:** Supabase Auth and Row Level Security.
- **Real-time:** live session tracking and automated reports.

## Results / what I learned

The hard part was real-time data and access control across four user roles in one system. It's open source and free for any institution to use.
